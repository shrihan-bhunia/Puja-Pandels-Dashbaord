ABOUT THE PLATFORM

About Pandal Yatra

Pandal Yatra is an interactive GIS-based web mapping platform designed to help people discover and explore Durga Puja pandals through an easy-to-use digital map.

The platform combines Geographic Information System (GIS) technology, interactive web mapping, and spatial data to visualize pandal locations and important information such as name, theme, address, and area.

Users can search for pandals, explore their locations on the map, filter them by theme, and view detailed information through the interactive map and data table.

Community Contribution

Pandal Yatra also allows users to suggest new pandals that are not currently available on the map. Submitted information can be reviewed and verified before being added to the official dataset. This helps keep the map updated and encourages community participation.

Our Vision

Our goal is to bring tradition and technology together by creating a digital platform where people can easily discover Durga Puja celebrations while exploring the cultural landscape through GIS.
